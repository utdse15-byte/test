# Product Ad Recipe

Define the product, claim boundaries, visible proof, opening state and final
packshot in the existing ShotContracts. Use the animatic to validate timing and
legibility before exporting any external handoff.

For H3 authoring, assign product identity/appearance to explicit references and
keep camera/motion in shot intent. Export one experiment at a time. Returned
media remains `unverified` until a human checks product fidelity, text, logo,
duration and ratio, then explicitly selects a take.
