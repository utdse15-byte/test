# Product Ad Recipe

Define the product, claim boundaries, visible proof, opening state and final
packshot in the existing ShotContracts. Use the animatic to validate timing and
legibility before exporting any external handoff.

Choose `portable_video` for a provider-neutral package or `minimax_h3` when the
strict H3 dialect is actually needed. Assign product identity/appearance to
explicit references and keep camera/motion in shot intent. Export one
experiment at a time. Returned media remains `unverified` until a human checks
product fidelity, text, logo, duration and ratio, then explicitly selects a
take.
