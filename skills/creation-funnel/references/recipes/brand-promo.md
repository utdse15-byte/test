# Brand Promo Recipe

Start from the creative charter and scene/shot contracts: audience perception,
brand assets, color/lighting commitments, pacing and final brand state. Validate
the whole rhythm with the existing animatic rather than treating a handoff as a
new renderer.

H3 prompts may compile only those commitments. Keep logo and identity ownership
explicit in references, preserve visible text verbatim, and separate each
creative experiment. A returned file is manual candidate media, never automatic
proof of brand approval, generator identity or Picture Lock. A human selects a
take only after the existing review and QC flow.
