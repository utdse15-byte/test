# Music Video Recipe

Lock the music source, lyrical language, edit beats, visual motifs and
performance ownership in existing project truth. Use animatic and temporary
audio only for timing validation; do not claim final audio or infer missing
music from any authoring profile.

Portable authoring writes absent facts as `Not specified`; the strict H3 dialect
keeps its `N/A` convention. Both preserve lyrics/dialogue verbatim. External
returns are ingested without automatic selection, checked for
sync/ratio/duration/identity, and selected by a human only after normal QC.
