# Music Video Recipe

Lock the music source, lyrical language, edit beats, visual motifs and
performance ownership in existing project truth. Use animatic and temporary
audio only for timing validation; do not claim final audio or infer missing
music from an H3 profile.

H3 prompt authoring preserves lyrics/dialogue verbatim and writes `N/A` when no
non-diegetic music fact exists. External returns are ingested without automatic
selection, checked for sync/ratio/duration/identity, and selected by a human
only after normal QC.
